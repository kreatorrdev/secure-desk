| Attachment | May download with valid rights | No right based on same company/role alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticket | Original Client / approved shared Client, assigned Staff owner/collaborator, scoped Admin, SA | Peer Client without share; Staff without concrete ticket access; other org.; Pending/anonymous |
| Proposal | Author, specifically assigned Staff reviewer with current assignment revisions, scoped Admin, SA | Other Client; Staff who only has company/can_create_project right; other org.; Pending/anonymous |

Proposal reviewer read/recommend does not grant upload right; upload is author/scoped administrator, active company and pending proposal. Final proposal decision disables new upload, and existing historical download remains tied to current rights.

Ticket/proposal native multipart endpoint and JS transfer JSON endpoint are checked separately. Manual UI rejection is not server-bypass proof. Reusable upload capability is only for quarantine; loss of business right must prevent publishing even if provider token temporarily still accepts write to dedicated quarantine path. Business reservation deadlines (15 minutes from migrations) and provider capability are separate. Previously saved/downloaded bytes cannot be revoked.
